Please be guided

Scope & guidelines

So there are no surprises on the day, here is what to expect from a Deep Cleaning.

Quoted or adjusted on-site

  • Items bigger than the listed sizes are quoted individually
  • Final pricing may vary based on the actual item size verified on-site
  • Pull-out, extension, or L-shaped sofas and pillows are priced as listed
  • Transportation fee depends on your location

Drying

  • Blow-drying is included in our cleaning process
  • 100% dryness is not guaranteed, as heavily soiled items may need multiple shampooing passes, which can affect drying time
  • Final drying time depends on the fabric and its condition
  • Please allow air-dry time after deep shampooing
Our Standard Operating Procedure (SOP): Before we leave, we ask you to inspect the work and approve it. We will address any concerns on the spot, within the agreed scope, until you are satisfied. Concerns after we’ve left are handled case by case and by approval, so please let us know the same day.
Discoloration note: Results depend on the fabric’s raw material, the item’s age, and the stain’s age and nature. Some stains or chemical-related discoloration may be permanent and cannot be fully removed through deep cleaning. We will still perform the agreed deep cleaning service, but in some cases, professional restoration or reupholstery may be needed.
