Scope & guidelines

So there are no surprises on the day, here is what a General Cleaning does and doesn’t cover.

Not part of the basic scope

  • Dishwashing
  • Hauling or disposal of items, decluttering, and moving or packing belongings
  • Areas above eye level or out of reach
  • Garage items, grease traps, and any extra steaming (available as add-ons)
  • Anything not listed in the inclusions above, which may be quoted separately

Quoted or adjusted on-site

  • Spaces of 61 sqm and above, and post-construction cleaning, are quoted individually
  • Actual size is verified on-site, and the final price follows the verified size
  • Additional fees apply for undeclared rooms, bathrooms, or work
  • Heavily soiled or neglected spaces may need extra time and may be re-quoted
  • Transportation fee depends on your location
Our Standard Operating Procedure (SOP): Before we leave, we ask you to inspect the work and approve it. We will address any concerns on the spot, within the agreed scope, until you are satisfied. Concerns after we’ve left are handled case by case and by approval, so please let us know the same day.
Please note: Cleaning results depend on the surface material, its age, and how long a stain or build-up has been there. Some stains, scale, rust, or damage can be permanent and cannot be fully removed. We will still complete the agreed cleaning service.
