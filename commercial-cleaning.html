Good to know

Schedules & after-hours cleaning

We can clean after mall hours for businesses inside malls. Let us know about any time limits or opening hours when you ask for a quote.

Booking & down payment

A down payment secures your schedule. For commercial jobs, the amount depends on the size of the job, and we’ll include it in your quote.

Outside Metro Manila

We accept commercial cleanings outside Metro Manila when the minimum total is at least ₱15,000–₱20,000, to cover materials, transportation and travel costs.

Our SOP: inspect & approve

Before we leave, we ask you to inspect the work and approve it. We will address any concerns on the spot, within the agreed scope, until you are satisfied. Concerns after we’ve left are handled case by case and by approval, so please let us know the same day.
